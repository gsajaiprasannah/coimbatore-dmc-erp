✦ THE WESTERN GHATS DIRECTORY ✦

Explore Destinations

Discover Ooty, Valparai, Anamalai Tiger Reserve, Siruvani, and hidden gems around Coimbatore.

2,240M ALTITUDE

Ooty — Queen of Hill Stations

Surrounded by misty blue mountains and endless tea gardens, Ooty offers cool pine forests, botanical splendor, and serene lake boat rides.

Distance: 88 km from Coimbatore
Best Season: Oct - June
Highlight: Toy Train & Doddabetta
Stay: Colonial Tea Bungalows
40 HAIRPIN BENDS

Valparai & Sholayar Dam

A tranquil plateau 1,075m above sea level with 40 thrilling hairpin bends, lush tea estates, and wild animal corridors.

Distance: 104 km from Coimbatore
Best Season: Sept - May
Wildlife: Lion-tailed Macaque
Dam: Sholayar & Aliyar
TIGER RESERVE

Anamalai Tiger Reserve

Protected tropical rainforest ecosystem teeming with wild Asian elephants, tigers, leopards, and rare endemic bird species.

Distance: 74 km from Coimbatore
Safari: Topslip Jungle Safari
WORLD'S 2ND TASTIEST WATER

Siruvani Waterfalls

Nestled in the dense forests of the Western Ghats, Siruvani is renowned for its crystal-clear water — ranked among the world's tastiest. The forested trail to the dam and falls is a spectacular nature walk.

Distance: 37 km from Coimbatore
Best Season: Aug – Feb
Highlight: Crystal clear dam water
Activity: Trekking & Rappelling
SPIRITUAL HUB

Coimbatore & Adiyogi

Home to the iconic 112-foot Adiyogi Shiva statue at Isha Yoga Center, Coimbatore blends ancient temple culture with modern spiritual tourism. The Dhyanalinga meditation dome and Linga Bhairavi temple are unmissable.

Highlight: 112ft Adiyogi Statue
Best Time: All year round
Also Visit: Marudhamalai Temple
Cuisine: Kongunadu Thali
OLDEST NILGIRI HILL STATION

Kotagiri & Coonoor

Kotagiri is the oldest and quietest hill station in the Nilgiris — perfect for couples seeking solitude. Coonoor is known for its 19th-century colonial charm, Sim's Park, and access to the famous UNESCO toy train.

Distance: 95 km from Coimbatore
Best Season: Oct – April
Waterfall: Catherine Falls
Viewpoint: Kodanad & Lamb's Rock
SEASONAL GUIDE

Best Time To Visit

Plan your journey according to the changing weather across the Western Ghats.

Summer (Peak)
March - June

Pleasant hill temperatures (15°C - 22°C). Ideal for sightseeing, flower shows, and escaping city heat.

Monsoon (Lush)
July - September

Lush green landscapes, roaring waterfalls at Siruvani, and atmospheric misty drives.

Winter (Crisp)
October - February

Crisp cold weather (5°C - 15°C). Clear skies, wildlife safari sightings, and cozy fireplace evenings.

Common Traveller Queries

Which airport is closest to Ooty and Valparai?▼
Coimbatore International Airport (CJB) is closest to Ooty (88 km), Valparai (104 km), and Anamalai. Our drivers meet you directly at arrivals.
Can we book Nilgiri Toy Train tickets through Coimbatour DMC?▼
Yes! We handle First Class toy train seat reservations for Ooty to Coonoor as part of our Nilgiri tour packages.
Do you offer customised packages for agencies and corporates?▼
Absolutely. We craft 100% bespoke B2B itineraries tailored to group size, meal choices, vehicle preferences, and net budget. GST invoicing provided.
What vehicles do you provide for mountain travel?▼
We maintain a fleet of modern, insured luxury SUVs (Innova Crysta, Fortuner) and minibuses driven by certified local mountain drivers.
How quickly do you respond to agency enquiries?▼
We respond to all agency enquiries within 4 business hours with a detailed day-by-day itinerary and competitive net pricing.
Best months to visit Ooty and Valparai?▼
October to June is ideal. March–June for escaping summer heat; October–February for crisp winter skies and wildlife sightings. Avoid peak monsoon (July–Aug) for mountain driving.
